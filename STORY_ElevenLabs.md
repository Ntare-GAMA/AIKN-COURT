# AIKN Court: Full Script, Music and Sound Design

**Runtime:** 1:30 (90 seconds). **Tone:** a warm, cinematic mini-documentary, from day into night.
Every start time below matches the Unity cinematic exactly, and the on-screen subtitles use the same words. Record the lines as written.

**Tips for ElevenLabs:**
- Generate each line separately and keep the best take.
- For shouted lines, raise **Style** and lower **Stability**. For the narrator, raise **Stability** so the voice stays steady.
- With the **Eleven v3** model you can add tags such as `[excited]`, `[shouting]`, `[laughs]` or `[whispers]` before a line, for example `[shouting] Take the shot!`. Don't type the tags in the subtitles; they're for the voice only.
- Check each clip's length against the **Max** column. If one runs long, raise the speed slightly or trim silence at the start.

## Music

**Style:** modern African instrumental: Afro-house / Afrobeats with amapiano log drums, at about **110 BPM**. It should feel hopeful at the start, energetic during the games, and triumphant at the end. **No vocals**, so it doesn't fight the dialogue.

One 90-second track, structured to match the story:

| Time | Story beat | What the music does |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Aerial title | Soft intro: warm pads, kalimba or marimba melody, light shaker |
| 0:10–0:30 | Visitors arrive, coaches | Light groove: soft kick and percussion, stays in the background |
| 0:30–0:48 | Day game | Full beat: drums, bass and talking-drum fills |
| 0:48–0:56 | Sunset | Breakdown: drums drop out and the sound filters down to pads; a dusky, magical feel |
| 0:56–1:20 | Night game | Big drop: deeper bass, log drums, more drive and tension |
| 1:20–1:30 | Win and final title | Triumphant swell, then one last hit on the title, then fade out |

**ElevenLabs Music prompt** (paste as-is and set the length to 1:30):

> Instrumental Afro-house track at 110 BPM for a 90-second cinematic about a basketball court at a university in Kigali, Rwanda. Start with warm pads and a gentle kalimba melody, then add a light shaker groove at 10 seconds. At 30 seconds bring in full Afrobeats drums, bass and talking-drum fills. At 48 seconds, a soft breakdown with filtered pads as the sun sets. At 56 seconds, a big drop with amapiano log drums and a deep bassline for a night game under floodlights. From 80 seconds, a triumphant swell ending on one final hit and a fade-out. Uplifting, proud, modern African. No vocals.

**Free alternatives:** Pixabay Music or the YouTube Audio Library. Search "afrobeat instrumental", "afro house" or "amapiano". Check each track's licence, and add it to the README credits.

## Sound effects

**ElevenLabs Sound Effects:** generate each effect below, then save it with the exact file name.

| File name | When it plays | ElevenLabs SFX prompt | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ambience_day` | Loops through the day scenes | Outdoor university courtyard in daytime, birds chirping, light breeze in trees, distant students talking | 20 s, loop |
| `ambience_night` | Loops through the night scenes | Warm African night ambience, crickets, soft breeze, distant city hum | 20 s, loop |
| `dribble_loop` | Loops while a player dribbles | Basketball dribbling steadily on an outdoor concrete court, rhythmic bounces, sneaker squeaks | 10 s, loop |
| `swish` | Each basket (5 times) | Basketball swishing cleanly through a chain-free nylon net | 1 s |
| `cheer` | Just after each basket | Small outdoor crowd of about 20 people cheering and clapping briefly | 3 s |
| `whistle` | Start of the day game and of the night game | Single sharp referee whistle blast | 1 s |
| `lights_on` | Floodlights switch on at sunset | Stadium floodlights switching on with a heavy electrical clunk and a rising hum | 3 s |
| `buzzer` | Final buzzer as the winning shot lands | Basketball arena end-of-game buzzer | 2 s |

**Mixing levels:**
- **Dialogue** is always the loudest.
- **Music** lowers itself automatically while anyone is speaking: it drops from 55% to 20%.
- **Ambience** sits at about 35% and crossfades from day to night with the sky.
- **Dribbling** is at about 50%, and only plays during the games.
- **Swish, cheer, whistle, buzzer and lights** play as one-off effects. Crowd cheers should stay small: this is a campus court, not a stadium.

## Putting the audio into Unity

1. Save the files in these folders:

   | Folder | What goes in it |
   |---|---|
   | `Assets/GAMA/Audio/Lines/` | `L01` … `L24` (the voice lines) |
   | `Assets/GAMA/Audio/Music/` | The music track (any file name) |
   | `Assets/GAMA/Audio/SFX/` | `ambience_day`, `ambience_night`, `dribble_loop`, `swish`, `cheer`, `whistle`, `lights_on`, `buzzer` |

2. Run **GAMA Court > Build Story Scene** in Unity.
3. Press **Play**. Everything plays at the right moment automatically.

Anything you leave out is skipped silently, so you can add the audio one piece at a time.

**Alternative:** you can instead export one fully mixed 90-second soundtrack (voices, music and effects together) and put it directly in `Assets/GAMA/Audio/`. It plays from 0:00 when the story starts. If you do this, leave the Lines, Music and SFX folders empty.

## Story summary

AIKN Court, an old outdoor court at the African Leadership University in Kigali, has been renovated. On a bright day, Ms. Uwase from ALU shows two investors, Mr. Habimana and Mr. Mensah, around the court. They pass the walls carved with portraits of Fred Swaniker and H.E. Paul Kagame and take their seats in the blue Intare section. Courtside, Coach Gatete and Coach Nshuti push their players, two ALU classmates: Kalisa in red #7 and Mugisha in black #23. Kalisa scores first, and Mugisha answers with a three. The sun sets over Kigali, the new floodlights and the ALU sign switch on, and the game gets louder. The coaches don't ask for perfection, only for the players to be themselves. Ms. Uwase explains why they invest ("not in concrete... in them"). Kalisa hits a final three to win 7–5, both players celebrate together, and the camera rises over the glowing court: *AIKN Court. Renovated at ALU. Where leaders play.*
