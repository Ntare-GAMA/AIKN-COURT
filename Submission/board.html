KEY DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS

  • Safety: 0.40 m risers and 0.85 m treads; aisle stairs at 0.20 m rise and 0.34 m going, with handrails on both sides; 1.1 m guardrails with mesh infill wherever the drop exceeds 0.6 m; slip-resistant treads with yellow contrast nosings; stands kept 1.8 m clear of the sidelines.
  • Durability: reinforced concrete with 30 mm cover; hot-dip galvanised steel; UV-stabilised seats that are bolted individually, so each can be replaced on its own; anti-graffiti sealer on the walls.
  • Sustainability: the existing slab is reused, not demolished; tiers are made of compacted fill inside block walls, which uses less cement; recycled-content seats; local Kigali labour and fabricators; LED lighting on timers; rainwater drains to planted beds.
Materials: reinforced-concrete stepped tiers on block perimeter walls · UV-stabilised polypropylene shell seats · hot-dip galvanised steel rails · slip-resistant treads with contrast nosings · LED floodlighting · carved render relief art · recessed IP65 LED signage · acrylic court coating.
